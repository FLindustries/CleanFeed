Clicked “Don't recommend channel” and it came back?

YouTube's “Don't recommend channel” and “Not interested” are feedback for its recommendations. They aren't a blocklist, and YouTube's help page doesn't say they keep a channel out of search.

CleanFeed keeps a list in your browser and hides the videos that match it as the page loads, so a blocked channel's videos are hidden on Home, search and Up next.

How it works

Everything happens inside YouTube

Block from the menus you already use. Change anything and every open YouTube tab updates right away, no reload.

Channels

Block a channel from the 3-dot menu

Pick “Block channel permanently” in any video's 3-dot menu, or press “Block this channel” next to Subscribe. Its videos disappear at once.

  • Hidden from Home, search, Up next, channel pages, playlists and Shorts shelves
  • In the Shorts player, a blocked channel's Short is hidden and paused, so you don't hear it
  • Also matched by @handle where YouTube shows it, which helps when a channel is renamed
  • Unlimited on the free plan

Keywords and topics

Block a word, or a whole topic at once

Type a word or phrase and videos with it in the title are hidden. Or switch on one of 35 ready-made topics, each with its own list of related terms.

  • Whole words only: “game” hides “Game night” but not “Endgame”
  • Works in any language and alphabet. In search, the description snippet is checked too
  • Topic word lists cover English and Italian titles. Switch off any term, or add your own
  • Gaming, Tech, AI Hype, News and Sports let some analysis, history and documentary titles through
  • 3 videos from one channel hidden for the same topic? CleanFeed hides the whole channel. Untag undoes it
  • Free: 3 slots shared by keywords and topics. Pro: unlimited

Find what to block

Pick words straight from a video title

Hover a video and click the green CF+ button. Its title turns into words and phrases you can tick and block.

  • “Not interested in this topic” in the 3-dot menu: block the video's whole topic, one word, or skip just that video
  • Scan, on a channel page, suggests up to 40 words and phrases that keep coming up in its titles
  • Words you add count toward the free plan's 3 slots

Shorts and comments

Hide Shorts and comments, one switch each

Hide Shorts removes them from Home, Subscriptions, search and the sidebar. Hide comments removes the whole comments section under videos.

  • Both stay off until you switch them on
  • In Subscriptions, CleanFeed hides the “More relevant” section YouTube's algorithm adds, so you see what your channels posted (on by default)
  • A Short opened from a direct link still plays

FAQ

Questions people ask first

Something else? Email factuallogic98@gmail.com.

Is CleanFeed really free?

Yes. The free plan has unlimited channel blocking, 3 slots shared by keywords and topics, Hide Shorts, Hide comments and every tool except Strict Mode and the Pro extras listed below. Pro is optional: it adds unlimited keywords and topics, Strict Mode, a log of the last 100 hidden videos and exceptions for channels you subscribe to.

Does it work on my phone, tablet or TV?

No. CleanFeed is a Chrome extension, so it works on youtube.com in Chrome on a computer. It can't change the YouTube app on phones, tablets, TVs or game consoles.

Why doesn't it hide anything in my Subscriptions feed?

That's on purpose. You picked those channels, so channel, keyword and topic blocks never apply to the Subscriptions feed. Two things do work there: Hide Shorts, and hiding the “More relevant” section that YouTube's algorithm adds to the feed.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no CleanFeed account and no sign-up. If you buy Pro, payment runs through ExtensionPay's checkout with Stripe, which asks for an email address for the purchase.

What does CleanFeed do with my data?

Your blocklists and settings stay in your browser. CleanFeed has no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send your lists anywhere. The only outside service it talks to is ExtensionPay, for plan prices and payments. The privacy policy has the details.

Does blocking a channel tell YouTube or the creator?

No. CleanFeed hides videos on your screen. It sends nothing to YouTube and the channel isn't notified.

That also means it doesn't train YouTube's recommendations: YouTube may keep suggesting the channel, and CleanFeed keeps hiding it. A video you open from a direct link still plays.

How do keywords and topics match video titles?

Keywords match whole words in a video's title, in any language and alphabet: blocking “game” hides “Game night” but not “Endgame”. In search results CleanFeed also checks the description snippet.

A topic is a ready-made word list: turn on Crypto and titles with words like bitcoin or blockchain go too. Topic lists cover English and Italian words, and you can switch off any term or add your own.

Can I move my blocklist to another computer?

Yes, with Export and Import in CleanFeed's Settings. Export saves your blocks and settings to a JSON file, and Import loads that file on the other computer.

Can I use it as a parental control?

Only loosely. Strict Mode (Pro) asks for a password before anyone can unblock channels, remove keywords or turn off Hide Shorts. It can't stop someone with access to Chrome's extension settings from turning CleanFeed off or removing it, so it isn't a full parental-control tool.

What happens when YouTube changes its layout?

Something may slip through for a while. CleanFeed works on YouTube's pages, and YouTube changes them often, which can break any extension like it until an update ships. If you spot a problem, use Report a Bug in CleanFeed's Settings or email factuallogic98@gmail.com.
