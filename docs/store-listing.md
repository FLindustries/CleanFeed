## Detailed Description

**EN:**

CleanFeed gives you real control over what YouTube shows you.

BLOCK CHANNELS PERMANENTLY
YouTube's "Don't recommend" button is temporary. CleanFeed makes it permanent. Right-click any video's three-dot menu to block a channel forever — across homepage, search, sidebar, and Shorts.

FILTER BY KEYWORD & TOPIC
Block entire content categories (crypto, gambling, politics, gossip, gaming, and 20+ more) with a single toggle. Each category automatically blocks related terms — blocking "crypto" also hides "bitcoin", "ethereum", "blockchain", and more. Add your own custom keywords too.

INTEREST BOUNDARIES
When you search for something new, CleanFeed asks: "Just now, Today, or Always?" This keeps one-time curiosity searches from permanently polluting your feed.

HIDE SHORTS
Remove all Shorts sections from YouTube with one toggle. Clean, distraction-free browsing.

SMART FEATURES
- Per-synonym control: fine-tune which terms each category blocks
- Subscription exceptions: auto-detect when blocked keywords affect your subscribed channels
- Hidden video log: see exactly what was filtered and why
- Multi-language support: English and Italian

FREE & PRO
Free: block unlimited channels, hide Shorts, 3 custom keywords, interest boundaries preview.
Pro ($3.99/mo): unlimited keywords, full interest boundaries, smart decay, hidden video log, subscription exceptions.

YOUR DATA STAYS LOCAL
CleanFeed stores everything on your device. No analytics, no tracking, no data collection. Period.

## Single Purpose Justification
CleanFeed has a single purpose: giving users control over their YouTube feed by blocking unwanted channels, keywords, topics, and Shorts.

## Host Permission Justification (youtube.com)
CleanFeed needs access to youtube.com to:
1. Read video titles, channel names, and descriptions to apply user-defined filters
2. Inject "Block channel" buttons into YouTube's video menus
3. Show interest boundary prompts when users search for new topics
4. Hide Shorts sections via CSS
The extension only runs on youtube.com and does not access any other websites.
