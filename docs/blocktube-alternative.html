A BlockTube alternative for Chrome

BlockTube is still on the Chrome Web Store, and it's free. If it isn't working for you, or you'd like something simpler, here's an honest look at the options, CleanFeed included.

Is BlockTube still working in 2026?

Short answerYes, it's still available on Chrome. BlockTube's Chrome version has used Manifest V3 since v0.4.3 (September 21, 2024), so Chrome's shutdown of Manifest V2 extensions didn't remove it. As of October 12, 2026, its Chrome Web Store page offers “Add to Chrome”, shows version 0.4.8, updated February 9, 2026, and about 100,000 users.

Some articles say BlockTube is a Manifest V2 extension or won't install on Chrome. Its Chrome manifest and its store page say otherwise. For context, Chrome disabled Manifest V2 extensions for all users in Chrome 138 (July 24, 2025) and removed the remaining ones from the store on August 31, 2026 (Chrome's timeline).

What has changed:

  • BlockTube's latest GitHub release is v0.4.8 from February 7, 2026, with no commits since.
  • The repository has 482 open issues and 18 open pull requests. It isn't archived.
  • Since mid-2026 some users have reported in GitHub issues that blocked channels or videos reappear (#682, #689, #704). Another commenter says it works fine for them (#693).
  • Recent store reviews are mixed. Of the 10 newest English reviews (August 11 to October 11, 2026), six give 5 stars, one gives 2 and three give 1. That's a small sample.
  • Two community forks appeared on Firefox in August and September 2026: ChannelSieve and YouTubeMyTube.

So BlockTube isn't dead. It hasn't had a release since February 2026, and some people are hitting problems. If it works for you, there's no reason to switch.

Where BlockTube is stronger

BlockTube has power-user tools CleanFeed doesn't. If you need these, BlockTube, or YTBlock, will probably suit you better:

  • Regular expressions in every filter
  • Blocking comments by user or keyword (CleanFeed can only hide all comments)
  • A video length filter
  • Custom JavaScript rules (“Advanced Blocking”)
  • Filters for Movies, Explore and mixes, and hiding watched videos
  • A Firefox version, with about 33,000 daily users (CleanFeed is Chrome only)
  • A password lock, added in v0.4.7 (January 2, 2026)
  • It's free and open source

Where CleanFeed is different, and where it's weaker

Different

  • 35 ready-made topics. One switch blocks a subject with its own list of related English and Italian terms. Turn on Crypto and titles with bitcoin or blockchain go too.
  • Whole words, no patterns. “game” hides “Game night” but not “Endgame”, in any language.
  • Blocking inside YouTube's own 3-dot menu, plus a “Block this channel” button next to Subscribe. (BlockTube's store page lists context-menu blocking too.)
  • Help finding what to block. CF+ turns a video title into words you can tick. Scan suggests words that keep coming up on a channel page.
  • Softer edges. Some analysis and documentary titles pass Gaming, Tech, AI Hype, News and Sports. “Watch just now” opens a filtered video once.
  • One switch each for Shorts, comments, and the “More relevant” section in Subscriptions.

Weaker

  • Chrome only, no Firefox version.
  • No regular expressions, no comment blocking by user, no length filter, no custom rules.
  • Not entirely free: channel blocking is unlimited, but keywords and topics share 3 free slots. More needs Pro.
  • New, with a handful of users, so it hasn't been tested by many people yet.
  • By design, blocks don't apply in your Subscriptions feed, and CleanFeed doesn't change autoplay or end-screen suggestions.
  • Like every YouTube filter, it can miss things when YouTube changes its pages, until an update ships.

Other alternatives

YTBlock

The closest to BlockTube in power: words and phrases with regex; channels, videos, comments, posts and playlists; Shorts, live streams and premieres; a channel whitelist; watched videos; password protection. Filtering by YouTube category, tags or description needs your own Google API key. Its site says it's all free, with no premium features. (ytblock.netlify.app)

UnTrap

A large toolbox with 250+ options: remove or redirect Shorts, hide recommendations and comments, block videos or channels, mute topics, keyword rules, AI video summaries, schedules and session tools. It has a free plan and a paid Plus plan. (untrap.app)

Channel Blocker

Blocks videos and comments by user or channel with one click, and supports regular expressions, so keywords are possible. It's free. Its last commit was August 4, 2024, and open issues include uploads with several channels not being blocked (#60, January 2026) and needing a page reload (#65, April 2026).

Unhook and RYS

Both hide parts of YouTube's interface, such as Shorts, recommendations, comments and end screens. They don't keep a channel or keyword blocklist. Unhook's “Hide Channel” hides the channel profile and subscribe area; it doesn't block channels. Unhook is free; RYS has a free tier and Premium. If what you want is a calmer YouTube rather than a blocklist, these may be all you need.

Video Blocker

The original Video Blocker, which blocked channels and title keywords, no longer has a working Chrome Web Store page. It's still on Firefox (2,215 daily users, last updated April 25, 2024). A different “Video Blocker” on the Chrome Web Store stops autoplaying videos on websites and isn't a YouTube filter.

Which one fits you

  • BlockTube works for you: keep it.
  • You want regex, comment blocking or custom rules: BlockTube, or YTBlock.
  • You use Firefox: BlockTube for Firefox, or one of its forks.
  • You want fewer distractions, not blocklists: Unhook or RYS.
  • You want one-click channel blocking and ready-made topics, without writing patterns: try CleanFeed.

Switching from BlockTube to CleanFeed

  • CleanFeed can't import BlockTube's settings. Its Import only reads files exported from CleanFeed.
  • Channels: block them again as they show up, from the 3-dot menu or the Block this channel button. Channel blocking is unlimited on the free plan. Step-by-step guide.
  • Words: type plain words and phrases into Custom Keywords. Regular expressions won't work, because CleanFeed matches whole words. Free has 3 slots shared by keywords and topics.
  • Try a topic first: one topic switch often covers a list of words you'd otherwise type out.
