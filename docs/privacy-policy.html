In short: There is no CleanFeed account, no analytics, and no ads. Your block lists, keywords, and settings stay on your device. CleanFeed contacts one outside service, ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com), for plans and payments.

What CleanFeed Does

CleanFeed (version 1.4) is a browser extension for YouTube. It is not affiliated with YouTube or Google. It lets you:

  • Block channels, so their videos are hidden
  • Hide videos by keyword in the title, or by topic (35 ready-made topics)
  • Hide Shorts, comments, and the algorithm-picked shelf on the Subscriptions page
  • Auto-tag a channel for a blocked topic when several of its videos match that topic
  • Keep a log of the videos CleanFeed hid, so you can see what was hidden
  • Lock your settings with a password (Strict Mode)
  • Export your settings to a file and import them again

Some of these features need a paid Pro plan. To do its job, CleanFeed reads video titles, channel names, and the description previews shown in video lists on YouTube pages inside your browser. That reading happens on your device and the text is not sent anywhere.

Data We Collect

From the extension: none. The developer of CleanFeed does not collect or receive your browsing history, usage analytics, or the lists and settings you create in CleanFeed.

If you buy Pro, the payment goes through ExtensionPay and Stripe. The developer may see the payment details those services show to sellers, such as the email address entered at checkout, the plan, and the payment date. The developer never sees your full card number.

Data Stored on Your Device

CleanFeed stores your preferences using Chrome's local storage API (chrome.storage.local). This includes:

  • Blocked channel names and handles
  • Blocked keywords and topics
  • Channels CleanFeed tagged for a blocked topic, the videos it counted for that, and channels you untagged
  • Topic terms you added or switched off
  • If you have Pro: subscribed channels you exempted from a blocked keyword
  • Extension settings (Shorts hiding, comments hiding, language preference, etc.)
  • If you turn on Strict Mode: a SHA-256 hash of its password (never the password itself)
  • A log of recently hidden videos (up to 100 entries)
  • Basic usage counters (channels blocked, videos hidden)
  • A saved copy of your Free or Pro status and of the plan names and prices, so CleanFeed does not have to ask ExtensionPay every time
  • Data saved by earlier versions of CleanFeed that this version no longer uses. It stays on your device.

This data stays in your browser. CleanFeed does not send it to any server, not even ours. If you use Export, CleanFeed saves a file to your computer, and what happens to that file is up to you.

Plans and Payments

CleanFeed is free to use, with an optional Pro upgrade handled by ExtensionPay, which uses Stripe for payment processing. It is the one network service CleanFeed contacts. Here is when that happens:

  • Showing plans: CleanFeed asks extensionpay.com for the current plan names and prices. This request carries none of your CleanFeed data.
  • Choosing a plan: CleanFeed asks ExtensionPay for a random key that identifies your install and opens ExtensionPay's checkout page in a new tab. From then on, CleanFeed checks your paid or free status with extensionpay.com using that key.
  • Payment record: ExtensionPay stores a small payment and license record for your install, such as whether you have paid, when, your plan, and your subscription status.
  • What ExtensionPay saves in your browser: ExtensionPay's code inside CleanFeed saves three items with chrome.storage.sync (or chrome.storage.local if sync is not available): the date it first ran, which it saves for every install when CleanFeed starts, including Free users who never open checkout; the key, once you choose a plan; and a copy of your payment record. The install date is not sent to extensionpay.com. If Chrome sync is turned on, these items may sync across the Chrome profiles you are signed in to.
  • Card details: Payment is processed by Stripe through ExtensionPay. CleanFeed never stores card details, and the developer never sees your full card number.
  • Like any website, extensionpay.com can see your IP address when your browser connects to it.

None of your blocked channels, keywords, settings, or browsing activity is sent to ExtensionPay. For details on how ExtensionPay handles data, see their privacy policy.

Permissions Explained

  • storage — Save your settings and block lists on your device, and the ExtensionPay items described above
  • alarms — Only to remove the daily alarm that versions before 1.4.0 created. CleanFeed 1.4 creates no alarms.
  • scripting — Right after CleanFeed is installed or updated, add its YouTube script and style sheet to the YouTube tabs that are already open, so they are filtered without a reload. Used only on youtube.com tabs.
  • Host access (youtube.com) — Read and modify YouTube pages to block channels, filter content, and hide Shorts
  • Script on extensionpay.com — A small script runs on extensionpay.com pages only, so ExtensionPay's checkout page can tell CleanFeed when a payment is done

Third-Party Services

CleanFeed does not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising services. The third-party services involved are ExtensionPay and Stripe, for optional payments, as described above.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone. The one outside contact is with ExtensionPay, described under "Plans and Payments".

Deleting Your Data

Uninstalling CleanFeed removes the data it saved in this browser. Copies kept by Chrome sync follow Chrome's own sync rules. Payment records held by ExtensionPay and Stripe follow their own policies. If you have questions about them, email us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

CleanFeed does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of CleanFeed after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
